What is AI 2027?

AI 2027 is a scenario written by Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland, and Romeo Dean of the AI Futures Project, published in April 2025. It is a month-by-month story of how AI development could unfold over the following few years, as AI systems become capable enough to speed up AI research itself.

The scenario is a forecast told as fiction, not a prediction of certainty. Its authors present it as one plausible path, so that people can compare it with what actually happens.

Why track it?

A scenario is only useful if someone checks it against the world. This dashboard watches five areas where the scenario would leave a visible trace: policy, the labor market, technology, public opinion, and the economy. Each month it asks whether the evidence in each area is moving toward the scenario, holding steady, or unclear.

The dashboard does not test every claim in AI 2027, and a reading of “accelerating” does not mean the scenario will come true. Read the scenario itself at ai-2027.com.
