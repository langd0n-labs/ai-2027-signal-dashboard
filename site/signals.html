Data sources

Each month the pipeline collects evidence for each signal from public sources that can return data for a past date:

  • Federal Register API: United States federal rules and notices that mention AI.
  • FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: unemployment, jobless claims, layoffs, productivity, wages, and labor share.
  • arXiv API: monthly volume of AI research papers. Thank you to arXiv for use of its open access interoperability.
  • GDELT DOC 2.0 API: worldwide news coverage of AI, and its tone.
  • Hacker News Algolia API: headlines of stories discussed by a technology audience. Used for headlines only, not as a measure of volume.
  • Epoch AI, Notable AI Models (CC BY 4.0): releases of notable AI models and their training compute.
